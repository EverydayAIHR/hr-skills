9-box talent matrix: blank template

Add your own people. Performance comes from annual review ratings, potential from five criteria rated 1 to 3. The grid places each person as you enter their ratings.
Provisional until your leadership has calibrated the placements
Performance score = average of Exceeds 3, Meets 2, Below 1 over the years rated. Potential score = average of five criteria rated 1 to 3, all five required. Chips show performance | potential.

How to use

  1. Choose Add a person, then fill in the ratings from their annual reviews and the potential assessment.
  2. The person appears in a box as soon as both scores exist.
  3. Change the cut-offs below if your company uses different ones.

Select all and paste into a spreadsheet.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Closing or reloading this page clears everything. Use your browser's Print to keep a copy of the grid.
